# UKHUPACHA UI — Reference Analysis

## Method and direction

The copied web-page examples were inspected before mockup composition. All 23 examples found are preserved in `references/`; Book Styles copies have a `book_` prefix, and the otherwise unnamed Essential image is preserved as `essential_unnamed.png`. The working direction is **Hybrid Discovery — Reference-Grounded Edition**: Flat Theme provides the legible structural chassis, while TravelBookLite provides recovered-knowledge surfaces, tabs, markers, page rhythm, and warmth.

## Strongest references

1. **`book_eaAPYF.png` — TravelBook Equipment**
   - Borrow: oversized banner; open-book dossier silhouette; asymmetrical master/detail split; status block; restrained ruled space; footer state.
   - Do not copy literally: medieval weapon taxonomy, cream-only palette, exact two-column proportions, or the pack’s “Equipment” title styling.

2. **`book_VuIjJu.png` — TravelBook Inventory**
   - Borrow: layered cover/page depth; projecting tabs; selected-slot emphasis; contextual popup; quick-slot rhythm; small peripheral ornament that never invades text.
   - Do not copy literally: potion art, RPG inventory categories, exact grid, checkerboard showcase background, or diagonal presentation angle.

3. **`P5+3nq.png` — Flat Theme composed interface**
   - Borrow: modular framing; compact controls; strong alert/action strip; one bright accent used as the terminal emphasis; varied panel scale.
   - Do not copy literally: blue/orange palette, generic settings form, centered demo-card composition, or stock sample copy.

## Combined UKHUPACHA language

The final system uses warm charcoal covers and cream/gold paper hierarchy from TravelBookLite, with Flat Theme frames acting as the Helix-built chassis. Green identifies verified Helix function and the dominant safe action; gold identifies important expedition knowledge; purple enters through anomalies, recovered knowledge, and “below” states; red remains reserved for health or invalid relations.

HUD elements stay compact and edge-anchored, using framed bars and slots. Menus use a larger banner/cover/page rhythm, varied scale, and offset groups rather than a centered stack. The puzzle terminal deliberately hybridizes both families: a strict Flat frame surrounds three paper evidence cards and a warm recovered-knowledge ledger. Ornament lives at corners, tabs, markers and separators; text interiors remain quiet. This is what makes the result feel composed rather than wireframed: nearly every large visible surface has a pack-authored edge, silhouette, texture, or inset structure.
